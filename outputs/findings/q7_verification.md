# Q7 verification: data-quality audit, cleaning impact and anomalies

**Verifier script:** `analysis/verify/q7_verify.py`. It was written from scratch and does not import or copy `analysis/q7_data_quality_anomalies.py`. It reads clean data through the `src/common` loaders and the raw parquet only for before/after values. It runs in seconds, sections 1–15, exit 0.

**Bottom line:** the arithmetic is excellent. Almost every count reproduces to the unit: firmware, test stations, telemetry, duplicates, tariff residuals, sub-70% SoH issuing, metadata, CSAT, funnel stage 1 and 2, lots, and connectivity.

The problems are in interpretation, and three of them sit in headline or "primary driver" claims:

1. **The "calendar-month failure regime" is a temperature regime.** Temperature in `city_daily` and `station_hourly` steps on the same days. The 1-Jul-2024 drop happens only in the hot cities whose temperature collapsed, not in the cities that got the same price rise. So the price "confound" is resolvable and resolved. Q7 currently contradicts Q2/Q3 (heat x charger generation).
2. **The station funnel is misspecified.** The generation effect exists only under heat. With a generation x hot-city term, the residual dispersion falls from 15.4 to 4.3. "0 anomalies" becomes 1 anomaly (STN-HYD-081).
3. **The hidden PARTNER pricing figure is framed with the wrong denominator.** tariff_code misses 100% of partner pilot pricing, which is 54% of all pilot-priced swaps. It does not "understate exposure by 43%".

There are also three smaller substantive points:
- The hero figure states the wrong tariff window. PEAK is billed at 12–13 h and 19–22 h, not 12:00–22:59.
- "Kyron 2W is supplier-wide" is not identified, and it conflicts with Q4.
- A material cost-model consequence of sub-EOL issuing is missed: ₹12.9M of wear is charged on packs already past end of life.

Verdicts: **10 confirmed, 7 corrected, 1 refuted, 0 unverifiable** (18 entries). → **needs_fix = true.**

## Issues that need a fix (most severe first)

1. **The time-anomaly driver is mis-attributed** (Q7.md #15, headline item 3, "Primary vs secondary drivers", DQ09, Rec 3, fig q7_07). The script never looks at temperature, but the temperature columns step on the same 1st-of-month days as failures. The analyst reads the abrupt month steps as a calendar artefact ("consistent with a simulation"). The synthetic weather itself is month-level, so heat *is* the month regime. The July-2024 "confound" is resolvable with a cross-city contrast. Labelling heat "secondary (+0.36 pts, n.s.)" contradicts Q2/Q3 and would mislead judges.
2. **The station funnel's stage 2 is misspecified** (Q7.md #13, fig q7_03). There is no heat x generation term, so the dispersion-widened limits come from a misspecified model. "No station stays anomalous" is an artefact of that. The rate ratios (Gen3 0.69, Wave2 0.76) average a heat-conditional effect. Wave2 is entirely Gen3 and only 7 Wave1 Gen3 stations separate the two terms, so they are weakly identified. The load term uses slots_3w, which Q7 itself flags as unreliable.
3. **The hidden PARTNER pricing denominator** (Q7.md #10, DQ02, Rec 2). "Understated by 43%" is wrong. Partner exposure is understated by 100%, and all-rider pilot exposure by 54%.
4. **The tariff window is wrong in the hero figure and text** (Q7.md #1, headline, fig q7_01). The bands are PEAK 12:00–13:59 and 19:00–22:59, OFFPEAK 23:00–05:59 and 14:00–16:59, STD otherwise, as Q5 documented. The 66% → 100% test is still true but weak. The true-band test is 11.6% → 100%.
5. **"Kyron 2W is supplier-wide"** (Q7.md #14, fig q7_08 title) over-reads the data and conflicts with Q4's "one bad batch" headline.
6. **The near-duplicate "blind by construction" reasoning** (Q7.md #4, proposed fix 2) is a non-sequitur. The proposed rule replacement is unnecessary.
7. **Minor inconsistencies:**
   - The teleport counts use truncated minutes.
   - The scorecard CSV text says "36,987 partial rows"; it includes the test stations. The clean data has 36,747.
   - Fig q7_05 calls D2 the "loosest rule", but D3 and D6 are looser.
   - Bengaluru's test-station sensitivity is +8%, not ±8%.
   - The heat effect pairs a weighted point estimate with an unweighted CI.

## Missed angles that would materially strengthen or change the story

- **M1. The wear-cost model charges packs past end of life (cross-question: Q1, Q4).** ₹12,896,314 of wear (₹105.5 per swap) is charged on the 122,204 swaps that issued a pack already below 70% SoH. That is ₹17.8 per swap across all May-25 swaps and ₹23.1 across all Jun-25 swaps. Under the brief's 70%-EOL, no-salvage book depreciation those packs are fully written off. Without that charge, cm1 per swap would be **+₹12.4 (May-25) and +₹18.0 (Jun-25) instead of −₹5.4 and −₹5.0**; Apr-25 moves from −6.3 to −4.6. A replacement-cost view keeps the charge. Either way this is the largest single sensitivity on the "eroding margin" headline, and Q7 found the issue but did not size it.
- **M2. Heat is the month regime, with a threshold.** City-month failure rates are flat below 34 °C (3.95%), then 10.07% at 36–38 °C and 15.3% above 38 °C. The cross-city contrast on 1-Jul-24 separates heat from price. Use this rather than calendar dummies alone.
- **M3. Heat x generation in the funnel.** Station-level failure differences are a heat-conditional design effect: Gen1 1.27 vs Gen3 0.44 in hot Apr–Jun, about 1.00 otherwise. Re-check STN-HYD-081 as the one remaining anomaly.
- **M4. The true tariff schedule as an instrument.** It gives a sharper firmware clock test (agreement 10.4% → 100% on n = 14,711 rows). It identifies the 16 mis-timed edge rows exactly, for pilot-city independent riders, so they could be fixed. It also shows the hidden PARTNER +15/−8 rows follow the same hour bands, which confirms they are pilot pricing.
- **M5. The synthetic weather is month-level** (within city-month SD 1.5 °C). Any within-month heat-day test is structurally uninformative and should not be used to rank heat as a driver.
